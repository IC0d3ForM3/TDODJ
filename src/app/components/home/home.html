Enter the Dungeon

A browser-based, turn-based dungeon crawler played from a first-person view. Build a character, descend into hand-crafted dungeons, fight monsters, collect loot, and find the exit.

Create Free Account See a Sample Game
🗺️

First-Person Exploration

Navigate 10 × 10 dungeons from a first-person view with a live mini-map. Discover hidden passages, trapped doors, and secret rooms.

⚔️

Turn-Based Combat

Spend your Action Economy wisely — move, attack, cast spells, or drink a potion. Monsters act when your turn ends.

🧙

Build Your Character

Choose from four species and four classes. Your stats shape every encounter — how far you see, how hard you hit, how much magic you can wield.

💰

Loot & Gear

Open treshers dropped by monsters or hidden on the floor. Equip weapons, armor, rings, and necklaces to boost your stats.

✨

Spells & Potions

Cast ranged spells against monsters, drink healing potions mid-combat, and pick locks with your Mind stat when brute force won’t work.

🏗️

Create & Share

Apply for Creator access and build your own dungeons — design rooms, place monsters, set traps, and publish them for friends or the whole community.
