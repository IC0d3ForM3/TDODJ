About The Dungeon of Danny Joe

TDODJ is a browser-based, turn-based dungeon crawler played from a first-person view. Build a character, descend into hand-crafted (or community-made) dungeons, fight monsters, loot treshers, cast spells, and find the exit — all from within your browser, no install required.

What Is TDODJ?

At its core TDODJ is a homage to classic dungeon-exploration games: narrow corridors, flickering torchlight, and the constant question of "what is around the next corner?". Every dungeon is a 10 × 10 grid of rooms and corridors rendered in first-person. You navigate with the keyboard or the on-screen pad, manage your Action Economy each turn, and make tactical decisions about when to attack, when to run, and when to use that last potion.

Characters are defined by their species and class — a range of stats rolled at creation determine how far you can see, how hard you hit, how much magic you can wield, and how well you resist enemy spells. No two runs are quite the same.

Features

  • 🗺️
    First-Person Dungeon Exploration

    Navigate 10 × 10 dungeons from a first-person view with a live mini-map. Doors, traps, hidden passages, and portals are discovered as you explore.

  • ⚔️
    Turn-Based Combat

    Each turn you have a pool of Action Economy points to spend — move, attack, cast, drink, or pick a lock. Monsters act when your AE runs out.

  • 🧙
    Four Classes, Four Species

    Play as a Human, Elph, DwarPh, or Shortie. Choose Fighter, Thief, Mage, or Healer. Your combination shapes every stat from Max HP to Magic Power.

  • 💰
    Loot and Inventory

    Open treshers to find weapons, armor, potions, spells, rings, necklaces, and coin. Equip gear from your inventory during the run.

  • ✨
    Magic System

    Spells cost Action Economy points and target monsters within range. Hit rolls use Mind + d12 against the monster's Magic Resistance. Lasting effects tick every turn.

  • 🏗️
    Dungeon Creator

    Approved creators can build their own dungeons — place rooms, traps, monsters, treshers, keys, text tiles, portals, and hidden doors — then share them with friends or the whole community.

  • 👥
    Friends and Sharing

    Add friends, share private dungeons with specific people, or publish publicly. Run the same dungeon as your friends and compare your SP scores.

The Story Behind It

TDODJ started as a personal project — a way to recreate the magic of classic tabletop dungeon-crawling in a browser without needing a group, dice, or a dungeon master. The name is a nod to that spirit: part D&D, part Danny, entirely its own thing.

The goal is to keep it accessible — easy to pick up, deep enough to keep you coming back. New story dungeons are added over time, each a little harder than the last. If you love it enough to want to build, just reach out and apply for Creator access.
