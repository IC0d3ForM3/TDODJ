How to Play

A new dungon is planned each week (and sometimes more). Start from the first story dungon, reach the End point, and then move to the next level when it becomes available.

Weekly Story Dungons

The story path begins with the starting dungon. You must complete that dungon by making it to the End point before progressing to the next level release.

If a new level is not out yet, keep improving your PC and play community dungons in the meantime.

Community Games and Publishing

You can play community-created games, publish your own dungons for friends, or open them to the public.

  • Play published community adventures.
  • Create private games for selected friends.
  • Share games publicly so anyone can try them.

Creating a PC

Build your Player Character (PC) in the Dashboard. Choose stats, gear, and a playstyle that matches how you want to clear dungons.

Your PC setup matters for combat, survival, and how fast you can push through harder levels.

What a PC Can Do in a Dungon

  • Explore using first-person movement and line of sight.
  • Fight monsters and manage turn-based actions.
  • Collect keys, treshers, and useful gear.
  • Use inventory items like potions to survive.
  • Reach the End point to finish the run.

Becoming a Creator

Want to build your own adventures? Use the Create tools to design layouts, place monsters, keys, treshers, and story text.

Then publish your dungon for friends or for the full community when it is ready.
