Try a Sample Game

See what TDODJ is all about before you sign up. Below is a published sample dungeon and the pre-built character you would take into it. When you're ready to play for real — create an account, pick your own character, and dive in.

How it Works in 30 Seconds

  1. Explore — navigate a 10 × 10 dungeon from a first-person view with a live mini-map. Use arrow keys to face a direction, Space or F to move forward, and B to step back.
  2. Act on your turn — you have a pool of Action Economy (AE) points each turn. Moving, attacking, casting spells, or drinking potions each cost 1 AE. When AE hits 0 it's the monsters' turn.
  3. Fight — hit roll: d12 + weapon bonus + Stamina vs monster AC. Damage: dice + Strength. Monsters fight back when it's their turn.
  4. Loot — open treshers (containers) on the floor or dropped by monsters. Pick up weapons, armor, potions, spells, and coin from your Inventory tab.
  5. Find the exit — step onto the Exit square to finish the dungeon and collect your SP (Skill Points) reward.
